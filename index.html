Our Story

A warm stop for cravings, conversations and everyday celebrations.

Masala Junction was made for the kind of visit that feels easy: a quick burger after class, pizza with friends, pasta for a comfort-food evening, or South Indian favourites with family. The food is fresh, the prices stay approachable, and the atmosphere keeps things comfortable without trying too hard.

Inside Purnima Talkies Campus, the cafe is close to the energy of Jhumri Telaiya while still feeling like a friendly corner where regulars are remembered.
